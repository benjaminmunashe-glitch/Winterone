Governance and sources

Who decides: Winter Watch only recommends; the Duty Engineer decides every night whether to treat, which routes and when trucks leave, and no truck is dispatched by this tool. What gets written down: the Duty Engineer records the decision and the reason in the night log before 16:30, and any ice report on a treated route goes to the Senior Engineer the next morning (plan sections 1 and 7).

  • Depots: tonight.csv (BDAIB hub, fictional practice data)
  • Routes and map points: routes.csv
  • Rules: plan.md (Winter Service Plan 2026/27)
  • Forecast: Open-Meteo forecast API, CC BY 4.0, no key, free for non-commercial use
  • History: history.csv, weather from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0)
