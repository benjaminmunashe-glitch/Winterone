# Winter Service Plan: Roads Team, County Cavan (fictional)

Version 2026/27. Practice material for BDAIB at NCI. The roads team, its routes and its rules are invented for teaching; the weather in history.csv is real.

## 1. Who decides

The Duty Engineer decides every night whether to treat, which routes, and when trucks leave. A decision-support tool may recommend; it never dispatches a truck. The Duty Engineer records the decision and the reason in the night log before 16:30.

## 2. Priority routes

- Priority 1: national roads and main commuter routes (R01, R02, R07, R08, R09). Treat whenever ice is possible.
- Priority 2: regional roads linking towns (R03, R04, R05). Treat when ice is likely.
- Priority 3: local and lake roads (R06, R10). Treat only when ice is near certain and salt stock allows.

## 3. When to treat (the 16:00 decision)

Use the forecast for the ground (road surface) temperature overnight, not the air temperature.

- Priority 1: treat when the forecast minimum ground temperature is +1.0 C or lower and the road may be wet (rain, drizzle, or a dew point within 1 C of the ground temperature).
- Priority 2: treat at +0.5 C or lower with the same wet test.
- Priority 3: treat at -1.0 C or lower with the same wet test, and only if the depot holds at least 40% of its capacity.
- A dry road below zero does not need salt: there is nothing to freeze. Say so rather than treating by habit.

## 4. When trucks leave

Trucks should finish spreading at least one hour before the ground is forecast to reach 0 C. A route takes about one hour per 20 km. Trucks do not leave before 17:00 or after 05:00.

## 5. Salt

- Spread rate: about 0.15 tonnes per km of route (20 grams per square metre).
- Depot capacity in tonnes: Cavan 900, Ballyjamesduff 420, Bailieborough 18, Cootehill 300, Virginia 320.
- Restocking is ordered when a depot falls below 30% of capacity and arrives in about nine days.
- Priority 1 routes always come first when salt is short. Keep enough for three Priority 1 nights in reserve.

## 6. Drivers

A driver works at most 11 hours in a duty, including the run. Two drivers are on call each night per depot unless the night sheet says otherwise.

## 7. After the night

Any ice report on a treated route goes to the Senior Engineer the next morning. A treated route that keeps icing points to a fault on the route (shade, a bridge deck, water running across the road), not to the decision.

## 8. Not covered by this plan

Snow ploughing, flooding, footpaths and housing estates are outside this plan. Questions about them go to the Area Office.
